# Vision

AgencyOS is a private, Windows-first operating system for a future entertainment representation agency.

## North-star statement

Every material person, relationship, asset, opportunity, transaction, obligation, document, financial event and strategic signal relevant to the agency should be representable, connected, historically traceable, searchable and actionable inside one coherent system.

## Product families

1. Identity & Organization
2. People & Companies
3. Relationships & Interactions
4. Talent & Representation
5. Projects, IP, Materials & Packaging
6. Opportunities, Pitches, Meetings & Submissions
7. Offers, Negotiation, Deals & Contracts
8. Rights, Options & Obligations
9. Finance, Commissions, Receivables & Ledger
10. Documents & Communications
11. Research, Signals, Talent Radar & Predictions
12. Workflow, Tasks, Approvals & Automation
13. AI/Agent Runtime
14. Executive Command & Analytics
15. Audit, Security, Policy & Administration

## Product philosophy

AgencyOS is not optimized for the average market. It is optimized for its owner and, later, the agency built around that owner. Native power-user workflows, dense information, keyboard control, multi-window operation and institutional memory take priority over consumer simplicity.
